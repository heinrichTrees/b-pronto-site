Overview

What B-PRONTO does

Optical satellites lose the forest to cloud cover for much of the year, and even radar at shorter wavelengths struggles to see past the canopy into the structure beneath it. P-band radar penetrates further, making it possible to detect disturbance and regrowth in Brazil's forests with a consistency that optical and C/L-band records can't match.

B-Pronto aims to evaluate L2 products from ESA's satellite mission: BIOMASS. Using product information on forest height and aboveground biomass, the project will quantify forest disturbance and regrowth impacts in Brazil's tropical forests. B-PRONTO will compare and validated the products against Brazilian field and reference data, focusing on disturbance and forest regrowth and move towards quantifying aboveground biomass loss and gains, respectively. The project will assess the suitability and applicability of the new satellite mission towards improving estimates of carbon loss and recovery and supporting national greenhouse gas inventories and climate policy.

Objectives

Four things this project will deliver

TRANSPARENCY

Compare and Validate

Compare BIOMASS L2 products (e.g. height) with field and other data, especially at regrowth supersites

DISTURBANCE & REGROWTH

Track regrowth

Characterize secondary forest regrowth trajectories following disturbance, distinguishing recovery from degradation using a combination of remote sensing products.

COLLABORATE

Working together with Brazilian partners

Share results throughtout project lifespan with potential end-users who may benefit from BIOMASS data e.g. NGHGI team for regrowth assessment.

OUTCOMES

Deliver for outcomes

Report updates to ESA and present results at international conferences and prepare research publications
